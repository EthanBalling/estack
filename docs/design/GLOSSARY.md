# Glossary

- **Workspace:** A selected project and its governing context and resources; distinct from a native conversation.
- **Router:** Essential governing instructions that direct task-relevant retrieval.
- **Owner:** The single authoritative destination for a fact, decision or kind of state.
- **Durable knowledge:** Evidence-grounded information expected to affect future work.
- **Task state:** Work status, blockers and next actions, distinguished from historical verification evidence.
- **Handoff:** Concise continuation context pointing to authoritative owners.
- **Migration:** Normalizing agent resources into the agreed architecture while preserving useful content and governing meaning.
- **Cleanup:** Removing or consolidating superseded operational records under preservation and recovery safeguards.
- **Adapter:** An optional bridge for a harness's discovery or loading behavior; distinct from canonical project knowledge.
- **Pending update:** An explicitly unmerged proposal; its existence does not establish accepted knowledge or policy.
